HyDE stands for Hypothetical Document Embeddings.
The generated text gives the embedding model more semantic material to work with.
For our project, HyDE is useful especially when the user’s wording is vague, informal, or very different from the instructor’s wording.

And also important: HyDE’s generated text is not treated as truth. It is only a retrieval aid. We never show the hypothetical passage as the answer.

So this failed output is actually a very good example of a HyDE failure mode: speculative expansion can poison retrieval if the generated hypothetical document drifts away from the user's intent.
