What Step-Back does
Query rewriting keeps roughly the same level of specificity:
User:
"where he gets params from url"

Rewrite:
"getting URL parameters, route parameters and search parameters"

Step-back asks:
What broader concept is this specific question really about?

Example:
User:
"why does useLocalSearchParams give me values from the URL?"

Step-back:
"route parameters and dynamic routing in Expo Router"

So:
Rewrite
→ better wording

Step-back
→ broader underlying concept
